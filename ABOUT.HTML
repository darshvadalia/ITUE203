ABOUT US

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT), located in Changa, Gujarat, is a NAAC A+ accredited and UGC-recognized private university.

Established in 2000, it features a 125+ acre green campus offering over 60 undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, pharmacy, management, and nursing.

Key Highlights: Offers over 60 programs across 9 institutes, with flagship courses in B.Tech, MBA, and Pharmacy.

Admissions: Based on merit and entrance exams like GUJCET and JEE Main.

Rankings: Ranked in the 151-200 band overall by NIRF and specifically 98th in the Pharmacy category.

Research: Focuses on innovation and industry collaboration, with dedicated research centers and labs.

Placements: Dedicated training and placement cells facilitating continuous industry interactions.

Campus Life: Provides comprehensive facilities including hostels, gyms, and sports amenities for a balanced lifestyle.

Community Engagement: Actively involved in social initiatives and community service projects.
